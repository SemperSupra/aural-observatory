Optional 3D spatial sound controller

Exploratory manual reference tone only. Move a source around your head using the circular pad, keyboard, or a connected USB/Bluetooth controller. HRTF spatialization operates on the existing C#-generated bilateral reference-tone WAV, not the randomized study, and does not change quiet-search scores.

3D spatialization is off by default and uses additional fixed digital attenuation. Use headphones for the intended binaural HRTF experiment. Ordinary stereo speakers mix left/right signals at both ears and may make 3D effects weak or misleading; loudspeaker spatialization is not qualified. The browser HRTF is generic, and reference-tone sweeps are not a validated localization test. This is not calibrated headphone SPL; keep device volume low. Moving sound cannot start playback—use the manual preview controls in this card to start it deliberately.
